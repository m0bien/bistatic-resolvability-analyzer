1. Spatial Gradients

The bistatic range gradient vector \(\mathbf{g}_R\) is the sum of unit vectors pointing from transmitters to the target:

\mathbf{g}_R = \nabla_{\mathbf{x}} R_b = \mathbf{u}_T + \mathbf{u}_R

The Doppler frequency spatial gradient vector \(\mathbf{g}_f\) accounts for the rate of change of Doppler shift with spatial position:

\mathbf{g}_f = \nabla_{\mathbf{x}} f_d = \frac{1}{\lambda} \left( \frac{\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{u}_T\mathbf{u}_T^T}{R_T} + \frac{\mathbf{I} - \mathbf{u}_R\mathbf{u}_R^T}{R_R} \right) \mathbf{v}

2. Resolution Metric Tensor \(\mathbf{M}\)

Combining range and Doppler sensitivities under the ellipse resolution model yields the metric tensor \(\mathbf{M}\):

\mathbf{M} = \frac{\mathbf{g}_R \mathbf{g}_R^T}{\Delta R_{min}^2} + \frac{\mathbf{g}_f \mathbf{g}_f^T}{\Delta f_{d,min}^2}

3. Separation Boundary Solutions

Resolving condition is \(d^2 \mathbf{a}^T \mathbf{M} \mathbf{a} \ge c^2\), where \(\mathbf{a} = [\cos\alpha, \sin\alpha]^T\). We expand this into double-angle variables:

\frac{M_{xx} + M_{yy}}{2} + \frac{M_{xx} - M_{yy}}{2}\cos(2\alpha) + M_{xy}\sin(2\alpha) = \frac{c^2}{d^2}

Solving analytically for aspect separation angle \(\alpha\):

\alpha_{crit} = \frac{\psi}{2} \pm \frac{1}{2} \arccos\left(\frac{c^2/d^2 - C_0}{D}\right) \pmod{\pi}

where \(C_0 = \text{Tr}(\mathbf{M})/2\), \(D = \sqrt{(\frac{M_{xx}-M_{yy}}{2})^2 + M_{xy}^2}\), and \(\psi = \text{atan2}(M_{xy}, \frac{M_{xx}-M_{yy}}{2})\).
